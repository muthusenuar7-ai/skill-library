Help

Finding a skill

Use Explore Skills. Type words in the search box - a skill must contain all of them in its title, description, tags, instructions, domain or subcategory. Pick a major domain in the left panel to open its subcategories, then choose one. Your search stays in place while you change the category; remove any filter with the × on its chip, or choose Clear All Filters.

Using a skill

Open Preview to read the full instructions. Copy Instructions copies the text for pasting into your AI assistant; Download saves it as a Markdown (.md) file.{% if download_enabled %} You can also download a whole category, your current search results, or the whole library as a ZIP.{% endif %}

My Favorites

The star saves a skill to My Favorites. No account is needed, so favorites are stored in this browser only: they are not shared and do not follow you to another device or browser, and clearing the browser's site data removes them. If a saved skill is later unpublished it simply disappears from the list.

Keyboard and accessibility

Everything works from the keyboard: Tab moves between controls, Enter or Space opens a category group, and Esc closes the filter panel on small screens.

Something missing?

Skills are added by the Datacense team. If you cannot find what you need, contact your Datacense administrator.
